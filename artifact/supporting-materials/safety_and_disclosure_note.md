# Safety and Responsible Disclosure Note

Most cybersecurity testing was performed on a safety-relevant radiation detection device in an isolated laboratory environment. Radiological validation was conducted separately at the medical school hospital under local radiation-safety procedures. The empirical work involved device-specific artifacts and validation workflows that could be misused if released in operational form.

## Why operational artifacts are withheld

Operational artifacts are withheld because they may include or imply device-specific configuration structures, sensitive management workflows, credentials/PINs, session behavior, logs, spectra, replay/restore patterns, crash-triggering inputs, fragile service behavior, or validation details that could enable alarm-behavior modification.

## What is safely disclosed

The paper and this package disclose a high-level assessment workflow, sanitized evidence categories, non-operational synthetic examples, CWE/CVSS-informed scoring rationale, defensive recommendations, and responsible-disclosure status.

## Disclosure status

The findings were communicated to the appropriate vendor stakeholders as part of a responsible-disclosure process. At the time of this revision, no response had been received.

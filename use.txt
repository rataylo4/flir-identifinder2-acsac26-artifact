INTENDED USE AND LIMITATIONS

This artifact package is prepared for defensive academic evaluation, evidence-traceability
review, and security research or education consistent with applicable authorization, law,
ethics, safety requirements, and license terms.

SAFETY AND RESPONSIBLE-USE GUIDANCE

The package is not an exploitation toolkit and does not provide operational scripts,
credentials, live endpoints, session material, proprietary binaries, or reproduction-ready
payloads. It is not intended to direct interaction with a live identiFINDER2 or any other
device. Users remain responsible for obtaining authorization and applying appropriate safety
controls before interacting with any system.

The released material is intended to support review of the paper's bounded claims, not to
enable operational interaction with deployed identiFINDER2 systems.

EVIDENTIARY LIMITATIONS

- The live assessment involved one physical unit running firmware 2015.1.
- The 2019.2 firmware evidence was examined separately and is corroborating only.
- The package performs static checks over sanitized derivatives; it does not reproduce the
  original physical-device experiment.
- Configuration-import acceptance is supported by researcher-maintained notes and the
  researcher-identified live-demo workflow, not by a preserved device-generated import log.
- The same suppress-script workflow was used for the NORM and SNM demonstrations by changing
  the classification value; no separately saved byte-exact SNM output is distributed.
- No trial counts, success rates, prevalence estimates, uncertainty measurements, or
  cross-device conclusions were collected or claimed.
- Operational implications are labeled separately from directly observed results.
